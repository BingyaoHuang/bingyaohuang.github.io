About

Bingyao Huang is an Associate Professor at the College of Computer and Information Science, Southwest University, China. He earned a B.S. from Tongji University in 2013, an M.S. from Rowan University in 2015, and a Ph.D. from Stony Brook University in 2021. His research centers on projection-based spatial augmented reality, differentiable imaging, and biomedical imaging. His work has appeared in IEEE TPAMI, IEEE TVCG, CVPR, ICCV, IEEE VR, ISMAR, and AAAI. He received the IEEE VR Best Journal Paper Award in 2021 and the IEEE VR Best Paper Award in 2026.
